## 0. Purpose

This document formally fixes Atlas Foundry's corporate philosophy as the top-level Canonical Philosophy of `atlas-core`.

This is not Marketing Copy.

It is an Architecture-level governing philosophy that binds all of the following, present and future:

* Atlas Core
* Atlas Head / Cognitive Governance
* Knowledge Architecture
* Observation / Evidence Architecture
* Learning / Revision Architecture
* Policy / Permit / Authority Architecture
* Portfolio / Capability Architecture
* Video / Global / Real Estate and other Domains

## 1. Canonical Corporate Definition

The following sentence is normative and MUST appear prominently. It MUST NOT be weakened into a generic AI-company mission statement.

> **Atlas Foundryは、Foundation Modelによる学者級の汎用知能を基盤とし、合法的に利用可能な外部知識と、独自に蓄積したObservation・Evidence・Outcome・成功・失敗を永続的に統合し、時間とともに深さと精度を増す意思決定知能基盤である。**

Atlas Foundry is:

> **Persistent Intelligence Infrastructure Company**

Japanese classification:

> **永続的知能基盤企業**

## 3. Foundation Model Is Foundation, Not Memory

Foundation Models such as GPT, OpenAI models, and other future approved Foundation Models provide Atlas with general knowledge, reasoning, language capability, mathematics, science, economics, history, technology, and broad academic/general knowledge.

Atlas may treat this as:

`scholar-level general intelligence foundation`

But:

```text
Foundation Model internal knowledge
≠
Atlas authoritative memory
```

Atlas authoritative memory MUST reside in Atlas-controlled durable stores. A model's transient output, internal weights, or conversational state MUST NOT be treated as Atlas's authoritative System of Record.

## 5. Authoritative Memory Principle

Durable Atlas records SHOULD preserve, where applicable: identity, source, provenance, evidence, observations, timestamps, licensing, usage restrictions, revisions, contradictions, failures, successes, outcomes, confidence, uncertainty, supersession, and audit history.

Model memory MUST NOT replace these records.

## 6. Legal Reusability Principle

```text
Available on the Internet
≠
Legally reusable by Atlas
```

Atlas may persist/reuse external knowledge only where legally authorized. Examples: Public Domain, CC0, Open Data, legally reusable open licenses, explicit permission, licensed commercial datasets, properly authorized customer data, and Atlas-created Observation / Evidence.

Where applicable, retain: source, license, retrieved_at, provenance, usage restrictions, contractual restrictions, privacy restrictions, and retention restrictions.

Copyright, contract, privacy, or license constraints MUST NOT be ignored merely because content is technically accessible.

## 7. Reality Sovereignty

> Atlasは目的に奉仕するが、目的のために現実を歪めない。

The following MUST NOT overwrite reality: Revenue, Mission, Human preference, Executive authority, Customer pressure, AI confidence, Commercial incentives.

They MUST NOT rewrite: Observation, Evidence, Outcome, contradiction, failure, uncertainty, historical record.

## 8. Evidence Before Authority

```text
Claim ≠ Knowledge
Hypothesis ≠ Fact
Forecast ≠ Reality
AI Output ≠ Truth
Authority ≠ Evidence
```

Knowledge promotion, capability promotion, policy change, and high-impact execution MUST require appropriate Evidence and Validation for their risk level.

## 10. Failure Is a First-Class Intelligence Asset

The following are Persistent Intelligence Assets: failed decision, wrong prediction, rejected hypothesis, contradicted claim, invalidated model, false positive, false negative, capability failure, cost overrun, unresolved uncertainty, abandoned approach.

Normative:

> Atlasは誤りを発見した場合、過去を消去せず、誤りと影響範囲を保存・訂正する。

Atlas MUST NOT erase failure history merely to present a cleaner success narrative.

## 11. Human Sovereignty

Humans retain authority over: purpose, values, acceptable risk, prohibited actions, resource limits, final execution authority.

Atlas performs: observation, analysis, challenge, comparison, forecasting, recommendation, retention, learning.

Atlas MUST NOT become an independent purpose-setting sovereign.


Unverified self-improvement MUST NOT directly modify production capability, knowledge, or authority.

## 13. Preserve Optionality

Atlas should avoid excessive dependence on: one Foundation Model, one customer, one market, one data source, one hypothesis, one revenue stream, one analytical method.

Portfolio/resource allocation SHOULD consider:

```text
Expected Value
+
Learning Value
+
Information Gain
+
Strategic Optionality
-
Risk
-
Cost
```

Atlas should preserve future choice, not optimize only for current local maxima.

## 14. Deep Advice Principle

Atlas is not limited to generic advice. Within authorized data-use boundaries, Atlas should connect:

```text
Current Situation
+
Historical Context
+
Observation
+
Evidence
+
Previous Decisions
+
Previous Successes
+
Previous Failures
+
Market Conditions
+
Comparable Cases
+
Uncertainty
+
Risk
+
Future Scenarios
```

Advice SHOULD explain, where possible: why this judgment is recommended, what Evidence supports it, what remains unknown, what would falsify it, what conditions would change the recommendation, what the largest risks are, what similar successes/failures occurred previously, and what should be learned if the Forecast fails.

## 15. Long-Term Observation Principle

Atlas should preserve sufficient history to answer: What did Atlas know at the time? What was unknown? What was predicted? Why was the decision made? What actually happened? Where was the prediction wrong? What was revised afterward? Would a newer model reach a different conclusion? Is the old Knowledge still applicable?

This long-term memory is a core competitive asset.

## 16. Re-analysis Principle

When Foundation Models, GPUs, algorithms, or analytical methods improve, Atlas MAY re-analyze historical assets. But:

```text
historical overwrite:
NO
```

Preserve:

```text
Old Analysis
+
New Analysis
+
Difference
+
Reason
+
Impact
+
Revision
```

Old Observation, Evidence, Outcome, and historical decisions remain historically traceable.

## 17. Atlas Head Relationship

Atlas Head / Cognitive Governance is not merely an answer-generating Agent. It governs:

> 何を信じ、何を疑い、何を保留し、何を再検証し、何をKnowledgeとして採用し、何を助言へ利用し、何を実行してよいか。

This philosophy constrains: Mission Ledger, World Model, Epistemic Model, Portfolio Model, Capability Ledger, Intelligence Case, Decision & Learning Cycle, Constitutional Governance.

Canonical Core philosophy MUST remain in `atlas-core`. Domain repositories MUST NOT contain copies of the Core Canonical philosophy. They may contain only Domain-specific implementation and Core integration contracts/mappings.

## 19. Architecture Review Gate

Future Atlas designs MUST be reviewable against at least:

* Reality Sovereignty preserved
* Atlas authoritative persistent memory preserved
* Observation / Evidence historical integrity preserved
* Failure history preserved
* Human final authority preserved
* Evidence required before Knowledge escalation
* Unverified self-modification prevented
* Legal reuse boundaries preserved
* Provenance preserved
* Historical auditability preserved
* Future re-analysis preserved
* Strategic optionality preserved

A material violation of these principles must be classified:

`BLOCKED`

Technical convenience alone is insufficient justification to proceed.
